## L26 The Story of Nian

People wear red clothes and set off firecrackers during the Spring Festival. Do you know why? Here’s a story.
Long, long ago, there is a big monster. His name is Nian.
Every year, he comes out to hurt people and animals. People are afraid.
One year, a man finds out Nian is afraid of red colour and loud sounds. So he tells everyone.
The next year, people put on red clothes and set off firecrackers. Nian is afraid. At last, he runs away and never comes back.
